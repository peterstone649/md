# 1. Validation (DEF_FOR_VALIDATION) **[PRIO: HIGH]**

*   **Validation:** The systematic process of confirming that mathematical structures, proofs, models, and computational methods meet specified formal requirements and achieve their intended mathematical objectives through rigorous verification and empirical testing.
*   **Description:** Validation in mathematics ensures that mathematical constructs are sound, proofs are correct, models accurately represent reality, and computational methods produce reliable results. It encompasses formal verification of mathematical structures, empirical validation of mathematical models, and systematic testing of computational algorithms. Mathematical validation prevents errors, ensures consistency, and establishes confidence in mathematical results and their applications.
