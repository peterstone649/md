# The Big Nine: How the Tech Titans and Their Artificial Intelligence Are Changing the World

**Author:** Amy Webb  
**Publication Year:** 2019  
**Publisher:** PublicAffairs  
**ISBN:** 978-1610398226

## Overview

"The Big Nine" by Amy Webb is a comprehensive examination of how nine major technology companies are shaping the future of artificial intelligence and, by extension, the future of humanity. Webb identifies these companies as the "Big Nine" - divided into two groups:

**The G-MAFIA (American companies):**
- Google
- Microsoft
- Amazon
- Facebook
- IBM
- Apple

**The BAT creep (Chinese companies):**
- Baidu
- Alibaba
- Tencent

## Key Themes

### 1. AI Development Trajectories
Webb explores how these companies are developing AI along different paths, influenced by their respective cultural, political, and economic contexts. The American companies tend to focus on consumer applications and commercial interests, while Chinese companies work more closely with their government's strategic AI plans.

### 2. Long-term Implications
The book examines potential future scenarios from 2020 to 2049, considering how current AI development decisions will impact society, the economy, and geopolitics. Webb presents both optimistic and concerning visions of what AI-powered futures might look like.

### 3. Governance and Control
Webb discusses the lack of effective governance structures for AI development and the concentration of power in these few companies. She examines how different political systems (democratic vs. authoritarian) approach AI regulation and control.

### 4. Technical Infrastructure
The book delves into the technical foundations of AI, including data collection, algorithm development, and computing infrastructure, showing how these elements give the Big Nine significant advantages.

## Critical Analysis

### Strengths
- Comprehensive overview of the global AI landscape
- Well-researched examination of both American and Chinese approaches
- Thoughtful consideration of long-term implications
- Accessible writing style for non-technical readers

### Limitations
- Some scenarios may be overly speculative
- Limited discussion of smaller players and open-source AI development
- Focus primarily on corporate and governmental actors
- Less emphasis on technical details of AI systems

## Relevance to AI Safety

"The Big Nine" is highly relevant to AI safety discussions because it:

1. **Highlights Concentration of Power:** Shows how AI development is concentrated in few hands, raising concerns about alignment with broader human values
2. **Examines Governance Gaps:** Identifies the lack of effective international AI governance structures
3. **Considers Long-term Impacts:** Encourages thinking about AI's long-term societal effects
4. **Cross-cultural Perspectives:** Provides insights into how different value systems might shape AI development

## Connection to Other Works

This book complements other AI safety literature by:
- Providing context for the "Big Nine" mentioned in Max Tegmark's "Life 3.0"
- Offering concrete examples of the governance challenges discussed in Stuart Russell's "Human Compatible"
- Illustrating the real-world power dynamics behind AI development

## Conclusion

"The Big Nine" serves as an important wake-up call about the concentration of AI power and the need for thoughtful governance. While not a technical AI safety book per se, it provides crucial context for understanding the social, political, and economic forces shaping AI development - forces that any effective AI safety strategy must account for.

Webb's work emphasizes that AI safety is not just a technical problem but a deeply social and political one, requiring broad societal engagement and international cooperation to ensure AI benefits all of humanity.
