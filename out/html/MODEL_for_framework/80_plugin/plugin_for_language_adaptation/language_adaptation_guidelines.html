📖 Language Adaptation Guidelines

Guidelines for effective language adaptation and translation management

🎯 Core Principles

1. Cultural Sensitivity

  • Respect cultural differences and local conventions
  • Avoid idioms, metaphors, or references that don't translate well
  • Consider cultural context when adapting content
  • Ensure content is appropriate for target audience

2. Consistency

  • Maintain consistent terminology across all translations
  • Use established translation memory and glossaries
  • Follow consistent formatting and style guidelines
  • Preserve framework structure and organization

3. Accuracy

  • Ensure translations accurately convey original meaning
  • Maintain technical accuracy for specialized content
  • Preserve important nuances and context
  • Verify translations with subject matter experts

4. Accessibility

  • Make content accessible to diverse audiences
  • Consider reading level and language proficiency
  • Use clear, simple language where possible
  • Provide context for complex concepts
