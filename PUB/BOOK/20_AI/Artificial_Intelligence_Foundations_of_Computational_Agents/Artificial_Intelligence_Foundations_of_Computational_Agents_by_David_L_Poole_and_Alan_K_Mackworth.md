# Artificial Intelligence: Foundations of Computational Agents by David L. Poole and Alan K. Mackworth

**Authors:** David L. Poole and Alan K. Mackworth  
**Publication Year:** 2017 (2nd Edition)  
**Genre:** Textbook, Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence  
**Pages:** 620

## Overview

"Artificial Intelligence: Foundations of Computational Agents" by David L. Poole and Alan K. Mackworth is a comprehensive textbook that provides a unified framework for understanding artificial intelligence through the lens of computational agents. The book presents AI as the study of computational agents that can perceive, reason, learn, and act in complex environments, offering a systematic approach to AI concepts and techniques.

## Key Themes and Concepts

### 1. **Computational Agent Framework**

**Core Concept:**
- AI systems are viewed as computational agents that interact with environments
- Agents have goals, perceive their environment, and take actions to achieve objectives
- Provides a unifying perspective for understanding diverse AI techniques

**Agent Properties:**
- Autonomy: Ability to operate without direct human intervention
- Reactivity: Ability to perceive and respond to environmental changes
- Proactiveness: Ability to take initiative and pursue goals
- Social ability: Ability to interact with other agents

### 2. **Knowledge Representation and Reasoning**

**Logical Foundations:**
- Propositional and first-order logic as formal representation languages
- Knowledge bases and logical inference systems
- Truth maintenance and belief revision
- Non-monotonic reasoning and default logic

**Practical Applications:**
- Expert systems and knowledge-based reasoning
- Ontologies and semantic networks
- Description logics for structured knowledge representation
- Constraint satisfaction and logic programming

### 3. **Probabilistic Reasoning**

**Uncertainty Management:**
- Probability theory as a foundation for reasoning under uncertainty
- Bayesian networks for representing probabilistic dependencies
- Markov decision processes for sequential decision making
- Hidden Markov models for temporal reasoning

**Inference Techniques:**
- Exact inference algorithms (variable elimination, junction trees)
- Approximate inference methods (sampling, variational methods)
- Learning probabilistic models from data
- Decision theory and utility-based reasoning

### 4. **Search and Optimization**

**Search Algorithms:**
- Uninformed search (BFS, DFS, iterative deepening)
- Informed search (A*, heuristic search)
- Local search and optimization techniques
- Constraint satisfaction and backtracking

**Advanced Techniques:**
- Adversarial search for game playing
- Monte Carlo methods and simulation
- Evolutionary algorithms and genetic programming
- Swarm intelligence and multi-agent coordination

### 5. **Learning and Adaptation**

**Machine Learning Approaches:**
- Supervised learning (decision trees, neural networks, support vector machines)
- Unsupervised learning (clustering, dimensionality reduction)
- Reinforcement learning for sequential decision making
- Active learning and exploration strategies

**Learning in Agents:**
- Online learning and adaptation
- Transfer learning across domains
- Multi-agent learning and coordination
- Meta-learning and learning to learn

### 6. **Planning and Decision Making**

**Classical Planning:**
- STRIPS and PDDL for planning domain definition
- Planning algorithms (forward chaining, backward chaining)
- Hierarchical task networks
- Temporal and resource-constrained planning

**Decision-Theoretic Planning:**
- Markov decision processes for sequential decisions
- Partially observable Markov decision processes
- Utility theory and multi-criteria decision making
- Risk-sensitive and robust planning
