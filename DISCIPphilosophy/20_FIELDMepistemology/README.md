# Epistemology Branch

## Overview

The Epistemology branch explores the nature, origin, and limits of knowledge, examining how we acquire knowledge and what constitutes justified belief. This branch addresses fundamental questions about cognition, belief formation, and the validation of knowledge claims.

## Key Theories

### **Classical Epistemology**
- **Empiricism**: Knowledge from experience (Locke, Hume, Mill)
- **Rationalism**: Knowledge from reason (Descartes, Spinoza, Leibniz)
- **Skepticism**: Knowledge limits and impossibility (Descartes, Hume)
- **Foundationalism**: Knowledge rests on basic beliefs (Aristotle, Descartes)

### **Modern Epistemology**
- **Coherentism**: Knowledge as mutually supporting beliefs (Bradley, Blanshard)
- **Reliabilism**: Knowledge from reliable processes (Goldman)
- **Virtue Epistemology**: Knowledge as intellectual virtue (Zagzebski)
- **Naturalized Epistemology**: Knowledge as natural phenomenon (Quine)
