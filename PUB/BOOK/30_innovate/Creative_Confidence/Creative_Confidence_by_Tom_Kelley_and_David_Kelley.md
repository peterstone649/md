## Overview

**Creative Confidence: Unleashing the Creative Potential Within Us All** is a groundbreaking book by brothers Tom Kelley (IDEO partner) and David Kelley (founder of IDEO and Stanford's d.school) that challenges the common belief that creativity is reserved for a select few. The book argues that everyone has the capacity for creativity and provides practical tools and techniques to help individuals and organizations unlock their creative potential.
