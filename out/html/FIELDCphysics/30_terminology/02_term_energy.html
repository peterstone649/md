Energy Characteristics

Fundamental Properties

  • Conservation: Total energy in an isolated system remains constant (First Law of Thermodynamics)
  • Transformability: Energy can change from one form to another (kinetic, potential, thermal, etc.)
  • Scalar Quantity: Energy has magnitude but no direction
  • Transfer Mechanism: Energy can be transferred through work, heat, or radiation

Energy Types

  • Kinetic Energy: Energy of motion (E_k = ½mv²)
  • Potential Energy: Stored energy due to position or configuration (gravitational, elastic, chemical)
  • Thermal Energy: Energy associated with temperature and molecular motion
  • Electromagnetic Energy: Energy stored in electric and magnetic fields
  • Nuclear Energy: Energy binding atomic nuclei and released in nuclear reactions
  • Rest Energy: Energy equivalent of mass (E = mc²)

Energy Relationships

  • Work-Energy Theorem: W = ΔE_k (work done equals change in kinetic energy)
  • First Law of Thermodynamics: ΔU = Q - W (change in internal energy equals heat added minus work done)
  • Mass-Energy Equivalence: E = mc² (mass and energy are equivalent)
  • Power Relationship: P = dE/dt (power is rate of energy transfer)
