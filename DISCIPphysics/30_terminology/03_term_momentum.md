# 3. Momentum (DEF_FOR_MOMENTUM) **[PRIO: HIGH]**

*   **Term:** A vector quantity representing the product of an object's mass and velocity, describing the quantity of motion.
*   **Description:** Momentum is a fundamental concept in physics that quantifies the motion of objects and is conserved in isolated systems. It plays a crucial role in understanding collisions, motion, and the dynamics of physical systems.

## Momentum Characteristics

### **Fundamental Properties**
- **Vector Nature:** Momentum has both magnitude and direction, following vector mathematics
- **Conservation:** Total momentum in an isolated system remains constant
- **Mass-Velocity Product:** p = mv (momentum equals mass times velocity)
- **Inertia Representation:** Quantifies an object's resistance to changes in motion

### **Momentum Types**
- **Linear Momentum:** Momentum of objects moving in straight-line motion
- **Angular Momentum:** Momentum of rotating or orbiting objects (L = r × p)
- **Relativistic Momentum:** Modified momentum at speeds approaching light speed
- **Quantum Momentum:** Momentum operator in quantum mechanics (p̂ = -iℏ∇)

### **Momentum Relationships**
- **Newton's Second Law:** F = dp/dt (force equals rate of change of momentum)
- **Impulse-Momentum Theorem:** J = Δp (impulse equals change in momentum)
- **Conservation Law:** Σp_initial = Σp_final (total momentum conserved in isolated systems)
- **Relativistic Correction:** p = γmv (where γ is the Lorentz factor)
