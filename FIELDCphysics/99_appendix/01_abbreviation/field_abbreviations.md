# Physics Field Abbreviations (FIELDCphysics)

**This document contains abbreviations and acronyms specific to the physics domain within the FIELDCphysics framework.**

## Physics Abbreviations

### **Fundamental Constants**
- **c**: Speed of light in vacuum (299,792,458 m/s)
- **G**: Gravitational constant (6.67430 × 10⁻¹¹ m³ kg⁻¹ s⁻²)
- **h**: Planck constant (6.62607015 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s)
- **ℏ**: Reduced Planck constant (h/2π)
- **e**: Elementary charge (1.602176634 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)
- **k_B**: Boltzmann constant (1.380649 × 10⁻²³ J/K)
- **ε₀**: Vacuum permittivity (8.8541878128 × 10⁻¹² F/m)
- **μ₀**: Vacuum permeability (1.25663706212 × 10⁻⁶ H/m)

### **Physical Quantities**
- **F**: Force (Newton, N)
- **E**: Energy (Joule, J)
- **p**: Momentum (kg·m/s)
- **L**: Angular momentum (kg·m²/s)
- **P**: Power (Watt, W)
- **W**: Work (Joule, J)
- **Q**: Heat (Joule, J)
- **T**: Temperature (Kelvin, K)
- **S**: Entropy (J/K)
- **U**: Internal energy (Joule, J)
- **H**: Enthalpy (Joule, J)
- **A**: Helmholtz free energy (Joule, J)
- **G**: Gibbs free energy (Joule, J)

### **Mathematical Operators**
- **∇**: Nabla operator (gradient)
- **Δ**: Laplace operator
- **∂**: Partial derivative
- **∫**: Integral
- **∑**: Summation
- **∏**: Product
- **∀**: For all (universal quantifier)
- **∃**: There exists (existential quantifier)
- **→**: Vector quantity or limit
- **↔**: If and only if (logical equivalence)
