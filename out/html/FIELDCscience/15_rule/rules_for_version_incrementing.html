Version Control Principles

Semantic Versioning Structure

V[MAJOR].[MINOR]
├── MAJOR → Breaking changes, fundamental redefinitions
├── MINOR → Definition adjustments, clarifications, additions
└── PATCH → Not used (corrections handled via MINOR)

Version Increment Triggers

MINOR Version Increment (V0.1 → V0.2)

Definition Adjustments: - Clarification of existing definitions without changing core meaning - Addition of examples, cross-references, or supporting information - Enhancement of field-focus-question frameworks - Expansion of practical applications or integration relationships - Addition of new types, subtypes, or classification categories - Improvement of philosophical integration or theoretical references - Enhancement of confidence assessments or validation criteria

Content Refinements: - Improved terminology consistency within definitions - Enhanced cross-references between related terms - Addition of new related terms or prerequisite terms - Expansion of confidence assessment rationale - Addition of new practical application domains - Enhancement of challenge/solution frameworks

Structural Improvements: - Addition of new integration relationship diagrams - Enhancement of field-focus-question frameworks - Addition of new philosophical integration sections - Expansion of confidence assessment frameworks - Addition of new reference categories

MAJOR Version Increment (V0.1 → V1.0)

Fundamental Changes: - Complete redefinition of core term meaning - Change in fundamental classification or type - Modification of formal definition logic - Alteration of priority level or scientific acceptance rating - Change in epistemological foundation or theoretical basis

Structural Overhauls: - Complete reorganization of term definition structure - Replacement of field-focus-question framework - Fundamental change in integration relationships - Replacement of confidence assessment methodology
