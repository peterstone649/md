# Legal Standards

**Version: V1.0.0** **Date: 2026-02-01**

## Copyright Standards

### Format
```
Copyright (c) [Year] [Organization/Author Name]
All rights reserved.

Licensed under the [License Name] License (the "License");
you may not use this file except in compliance with the License.
You may obtain a copy of the License at [URL]

Unless required by applicable law or agreed to in writing, software
distributed under the License is distributed on an "AS IS" BASIS,
WITHOUT WARRANTIES OR CONDITIONS OF ANY KIND, either express or implied.
See the License for the specific language governing permissions and
limitations under the License.
```

### Requirements
- Include full copyright notice in all source files
- Maintain consistent year format (YYYY)
- Use standard license headers
- Include applicable jurisdiction information

## License Standards

### Acceptable Licenses
- EUPL v1.2 (Primary framework license)
- MIT License
- Apache License 2.0
- BSD 3-Clause License
- Other OSI-approved licenses (with approval)

### License Compatibility
- All licenses must be compatible with EUPL v1.2
- Dual-licensing requires legal review
- Third-party dependencies must be compatible

## Terms of Service Standards

### Required Sections
1. Introduction and scope
2. Definitions
3. User obligations
4. Intellectual property rights
5. Privacy and data protection
6. Limitation of liability
7. Governing law and jurisdiction
8. Changes to terms

### Format Requirements
- Clear, readable language
- Version numbering system
- Effective date specification
- Contact information for questions

## Privacy Standards

### Data Protection Principles
- Data minimization
- Purpose limitation
- Storage limitation
- Accuracy
- Integrity and confidentiality

### Required Elements
- Data collection purposes
- Data retention periods
- User rights information
- Contact details for data protection
- Third-party data sharing disclosure

## Compliance Requirements

### Regular Reviews
- Annual legal compliance review
- License compatibility verification
- Privacy policy updates as needed
- Terms of service review and updates

### Documentation
- Maintain legal document version history
- Track license changes and compatibility
- Document compliance review results
- Keep legal consultation records
