1. Multi-Species Priority Framework

Lexical Hierarchy Structure

Tier 1: Ecosystem Survival (Non-negotiable) - Ecosystem Integrity: Functioning of critical ecosystem processes - Keystone Species Protection: Species essential for ecosystem stability - Genetic Diversity Preservation: Essential genetic material for species survival - Climate Regulation: Ecosystems' role in global climate stability

Tier 2: Conscious Species Protection (High Priority) - High Consciousness Species: Mammals, birds, cephalopods with self-awareness - Social Complexity: Species with advanced social structures and relationships - Pain Perception: Species with demonstrated pain response and suffering capacity - Intelligence and Learning: Species with advanced cognitive capabilities

Tier 3: Biodiversity Maintenance (Medium Priority) - Species Diversity: Overall species count and variety preservation - Habitat Diversity: Variety of ecosystem types and niches - Functional Diversity: Range of ecological roles and functions - Population Viability: Minimum viable population sizes for all species

Tier 4: Human Development (Lower Priority) - Sustainable Development: Human needs met within ecological limits - Economic Activity: Economic systems compatible with biodiversity - Cultural Values: Human cultural practices that support conservation - Technological Progress: Innovation that enhances rather than harms biodiversity
