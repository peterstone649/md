04. Model (DEF_FOR_MODEL) [PRIO: HIGH]

Version: V0.1 Date: 2026-01-07

  • Model: A simplified representation of a complex system or phenomenon that captures essential features and relationships, enabling understanding, prediction, and manipulation of reality through abstraction and idealization.
  • Description: Models are cognitive and computational tools that simplify complex reality to make it comprehensible and actionable. They abstract away irrelevant details while preserving key structural and functional relationships, allowing us to reason about systems that would otherwise be too complex to understand directly. Models serve as bridges between theory and observation, enabling prediction, explanation, and control. Without models, our ability to understand and interact with complex systems would be severely limited.
