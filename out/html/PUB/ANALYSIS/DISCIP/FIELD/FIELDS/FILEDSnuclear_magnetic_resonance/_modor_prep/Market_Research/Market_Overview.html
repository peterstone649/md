Executive Summary

The portable magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) market represents a rapidly growing segment within the broader medical imaging industry. Driven by technological advancements, increasing demand for point-of-care diagnostics, and the need for more accessible imaging solutions, the portable MRI market is experiencing significant growth and innovation.

Market Definition

Portable MRI Systems are compact, mobile magnetic resonance imaging devices designed to provide diagnostic imaging capabilities in non-traditional settings such as bedside, emergency departments, intensive care units, and remote locations. These systems typically operate at lower magnetic field strengths (0.064T to 0.3T) compared to traditional fixed MRI systems (1.5T to 3.0T).
