# Getting Started with MFW

Welcome to the Model for Framework! We're excited to have you on board.

## Quick Setup

Setting up your first framework is as easy as 1-2-3:

1. **Install**: Run `npm install mfw-core` in your terminal
2. **Initialize**: Execute `mfw init my-awesome-framework`
3. **Start Building**: You're all set to create amazing frameworks!

## Community Support

Our community is super friendly and always ready to help. Don't be shy - jump into our Discord server and say hello! We've got channels for everything from beginner questions to advanced framework architecture.

Here are some pro tips to get you started:

- **Keep it simple**: Don't over-engineer your first framework
- **Test early**: Write tests as you go, not after
- **Document everything**: Your future self will thank you
- **Ask for help**: We're all learning together

  • Arabic: Polite, honorific, community-oriented

🎯 Cultural Sensitivity Guidelines

Do's

  • Research cultural norms: Understand target culture communication styles
  • Use appropriate formality: Match formality level to cultural expectations
  • Adapt examples: Use locally relevant examples and references
  • Consider hierarchy: Respect cultural hierarchies and social structures
  • Be inclusive: Ensure content is welcoming to all cultural backgrounds

Don'ts

  • Avoid stereotypes: Don't rely on cultural clichés or assumptions
  • Don't force direct translation: Adapt meaning, not just words
  • Avoid cultural bias: Present information neutrally
  • Don't ignore religious considerations: Be mindful of religious sensitivities
  • Don't assume universality: What works in one culture may not work in another
