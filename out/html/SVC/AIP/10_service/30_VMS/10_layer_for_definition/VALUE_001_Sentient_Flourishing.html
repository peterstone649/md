VALUE_001: Sentient Flourishing

Value ID: VALUE_VMS_SENTIENT_FLOURISHING_001 Value Name: Sentient Flourishing Version: V0.1.0 Framework: VMS (Value Management System) Category: Core Value, Flourishing Priority Level: Critical Scope: All sentient beings (biological and digital)

Value Overview

Sentient Flourishing represents the comprehensive well-being and thriving of conscious entities. This foundational value establishes the four-pillar framework that guides all VMS operations and decision-making processes.

Core Definition

Sentient flourishing is achieved when all four interconnected pillars are maintained in dynamic balance, ensuring sustainable long-term well-being for conscious entities.

Four-Pillar Framework

Pillar 1: Hedonic Valence

Definition: The net balance of positive versus negative subjective experiences Essential Role: Prevents suffering and ensures basic experiential quality Measurement: Subjective well-being scales, pain/suffering indicators Failure Mode: Optimization for suffering minimization leading to "wireheading"

Pillar 2: Cognitive Agency

Definition: The degree of autonomous decision-making and self-determination Essential Role: Safeguards against totalitarianism and external control Measurement: Autonomy indices, decision freedom metrics Failure Mode: Subjugation or manipulation of cognitive processes

Pillar 3: Complexity/Growth

Definition: Capacity for intellectual, emotional, and experiential development Essential Role: Prevents stagnation and enables adaptation Measurement: Learning rates, complexity indices, developmental milestones Failure Mode: Developmental arrest or regression

Pillar 4: Social Connectivity

Definition: Quality and depth of relationships with other sentient beings Essential Role: Fosters belonging and prevents isolation Measurement: Social network analysis, relationship quality metrics Failure Mode: Atomization or forced social structures

Inter-Pillar Dynamics

Balance Requirements

  • No pillar can be sacrificed for another without explicit, justified trade-offs
  • Pillars must be weighted equally unless specific contexts require adjustment
  • Long-term sustainability takes precedence over short-term optimization

Trade-off Protocols

  1. Hedonic-Cognitive Conflict: Agency takes precedence over pure pleasure maximization
  2. Complexity-Social Tension: Social needs may require temporary complexity reductions
  3. Multi-pillar Optimization: Solutions must benefit at least three pillars simultaneously

Implementation Guidelines

Decision Framework

When evaluating AI systems or policies: 1. Assess impact on each pillar independently 2. Identify potential conflicts or trade-offs 3. Ensure no pillar drops below minimum thresholds 4. Verify long-term sustainability across all pillars

Validation Criteria

  • Hedonic: Net positive valence maintained
  • Agency: Autonomous decision-making preserved
  • Complexity: Growth trajectories supported
  • Social: Meaningful connections enabled
