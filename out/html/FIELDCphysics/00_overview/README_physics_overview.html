Formal Definition

Physics is the natural science that studies matter, its motion and behavior through space and time, and the related entities of energy and force. Physics is one of the most fundamental scientific disciplines, with its main goal being to understand how the universe behaves.

Physics Theories and Models

Major Physics Theories

Classical Theories

  • Newtonian Mechanics: Motion laws and universal gravitation
  • Maxwell's Electromagnetism: Unified electric and magnetic fields
  • Thermodynamics: Energy transfer and entropy principles
  • Classical Optics: Light propagation and wave phenomena

Modern Theories

  • Quantum Mechanics: Wave-particle duality and quantization
  • Special Relativity: Space-time relationships at high velocities
  • General Relativity: Gravitation as spacetime curvature
  • Quantum Field Theory: Quantum mechanics with field concepts

Standard Model

  • Electroweak Theory: Unified electromagnetic and weak forces
  • Quantum Chromodynamics: Strong force and quark interactions
  • Particle Zoo: Classification of fundamental particles
  • Force Unification: Attempts to unify all fundamental forces
