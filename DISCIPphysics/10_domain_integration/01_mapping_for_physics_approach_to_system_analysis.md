## Overview

The physics approach to system analysis provides a rigorous framework for understanding, modeling, and predicting the behavior of complex systems through the application of fundamental physical principles and mathematical methods.

## Physics Principles for System Analysis

### **1. Conservation Laws**
- **Conservation of Energy**: Total energy in isolated systems remains constant
- **Conservation of Momentum**: Total momentum in isolated systems remains constant
- **Conservation of Mass**: Mass cannot be created or destroyed in classical systems
- **Conservation of Charge**: Electric charge is conserved in all physical processes

### **2. Equilibrium Principles**
- **Thermodynamic Equilibrium**: Systems tend toward minimum energy states
- **Mechanical Equilibrium**: Net forces and torques equal zero
- **Chemical Equilibrium**: Reaction rates balance in reversible processes
- **Statistical Equilibrium**: Macroscopic properties remain constant over time

### **3. Causality and Determinism**
- **Cause and Effect**: Every effect has a physical cause
- **Deterministic Evolution**: System state evolves predictably according to physical laws
- **Initial Conditions**: System behavior depends on initial state and boundary conditions
- **Predictive Power**: Physical laws enable accurate predictions of future states
