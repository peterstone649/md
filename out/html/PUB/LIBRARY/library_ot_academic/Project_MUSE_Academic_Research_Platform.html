Project MUSE: A Comprehensive Analysis of the Academic Research Platform

Overview

Project MUSE (Project Muse) is a leading provider of digital humanities and social sciences content, founded in 1993 by Johns Hopkins University Press. It serves as a trusted source of complete, full-text versions of scholarly journals from leading university presses and scholarly societies, offering researchers and students access to high-quality academic content across multiple disciplines.

Historical Development

Founding and Early Years (1993-2000)

  • Founded: 1993 by Johns Hopkins University Press
