01. Thesis of Behavioral Surplus [THESIS_BEHAVIORAL_SURPLUS] [PRIO: MAXIMUM]

Version: V1.0.0 Date: 2026-01-31

  • Thesis: Behavioral surplus is the foundational raw material of surveillance capitalism, representing the unilateral extraction of human experience data without meaningful consent, creating unprecedented concentrations of knowledge and power that threaten individual autonomy and democratic institutions.
  • Description: The behavioral surplus thesis establishes that surveillance capitalism's economic logic depends on the extraction of vast quantities of behavioral data from human experience, treating human lives as raw material for commercial exploitation through prediction and behavioral modification.
  • Formal Statement: ∀c∃d∃p∃m (SurveillanceCapitalism(c) → ∃d∃p∃m (BehavioralSurplus(d) ∧ PredictionProducts(p) ∧ BehavioralMarkets(m) ∧ PowerConcentration(c,d,p,m)))
  • Scientific Foundation: Based on analysis of digital platform business models, data extraction practices, economic value chains, and the transformation of human behavior into commodifiable data assets, supported by examination of corporate practices and technological infrastructure.
  • Implications: Human experience becomes economic resource; privacy is fundamentally compromised; individual autonomy is threatened; democratic institutions face unprecedented challenges from corporate knowledge asymmetries.
  • Applications: Data protection policy, privacy regulation, antitrust enforcement, democratic oversight of technology, alternative economic models for digital platforms.
  • Consequence: Unchecked behavioral surplus extraction leads to corporate control over human behavior and democratic erosion; effective regulation preserves human autonomy and democratic values.
