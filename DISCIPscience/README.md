## Core Scientific Methodology Framework

### **Epistemological Hierarchy**

The Science field establishes a comprehensive epistemological hierarchy that structures scientific inquiry from initial conception through validation and application:

```
Scientific Method Flow:
Rationale → Hypothesis → Theory → Observation → Evidence → Experiment → Variable → Causality
    ↓           ↓           ↓           ↓           ↓           ↓           ↓           ↓
Justification  Prediction  Explanation  Data       Validation  Testing     Measurement  Inference
```

### **Critical Components**

#### **EVIDENCE (18_term_evidence.md)** **[CRITICAL]**
- **Epistemological Core:** Foundation of all scientific claims and rational belief
- **Key Function:** Bridges observation to conclusion through empirical validation
- **Types:** Direct, Indirect, Circumstantial, Convergent, Counter-evidence
- **Applications:** Research validation, policy development, clinical decision-making

#### **EXPERIMENT (19_term_experiment.md)** **[CRITICAL]**
- **Primary Mechanism:** Systematic testing of hypotheses through controlled manipulation
- **Key Function:** Establishes causal relationships and validates predictions
- **Types:** Controlled, Field, Natural, Quasi-experimental, Longitudinal
- **Applications:** Hypothesis testing, mechanism discovery, intervention evaluation

#### **VARIABLE (20_term_variable.md)** **[HIGH]**
- **Measurement Framework:** Defines measurable factors in research and analysis
- **Key Function:** Operationalizes concepts for empirical investigation
- **Types:** Independent, Dependent, Control, Mediating, Moderating, Confounding
- **Applications:** Experimental design, statistical analysis, causal modeling

#### **CAUSALITY (21_term_causality.md)** **[HIGH]**
- **Explanatory Foundation:** Establishes cause-effect relationships and mechanisms
- **Key Function:** Provides systematic understanding of why phenomena occur
- **Types:** Direct, Indirect, Necessary, Sufficient, Probabilistic, Contributory
- **Applications:** Mechanism discovery, risk assessment, intervention design

### **Supporting Framework**

#### **HYPOTHESIS (13_term_hypothesis.md)** **[HIGH]**
- **Predictive Engine:** Testable propositions linking variables and outcomes
- **Types:** Research, Null, Alternative, Directional, Non-directional, Complex
- **Philosophical Integration:** Popper's falsifiability, hypothetico-deductive method

#### **THEORY (14_term_theory.md)** **[HIGH]**
- **Explanatory Systems:** Comprehensive frameworks organizing empirical knowledge
- **Types:** Scientific, Substantive, Formal, Grounded, Middle-range, Paradigm
- **Philosophical Integration:** Scientific realism, theory change, Kuhn's paradigms

#### **OBSERVATION (15_term_observation.md)** **[CRITICAL]**
- **Data Foundation:** Systematic empirical data collection and recording
- **Types:** Naturalistic, Controlled, Participant, Non-participant, Structured
- **Quality Standards:** Reliability, validity, objectivity, systematic documentation

#### **RATIONALE (12_term_rationale.md)** **[MEDIUM-HIGH]**
- **Justification Framework:** Establishes logical basis for research and investigation
- **Types:** Theoretical, Practical, Exploratory, Confirmatory, Applied
- **Integration:** Research ethics, resource allocation, knowledge advancement

#### **ASPECT (16_term_aspect.md)** **[MEDIUM-HIGH]**
- **Analytical Perspectives:** Multi-dimensional analysis of phenomena
- **Types:** Temporal, Spatial, Causal, Systemic, Functional, Structural
- **Applications:** Complex problem analysis, interdisciplinary integration

#### **FOCUS (17_term_focus.md)** **[MEDIUM-HIGH]**
- **Concentration Framework:** Directed resource allocation and attention management
- **Types:** Narrow, Broad, Sequential, Parallel, Dynamic, Sustained
- **Applications:** Research prioritization, efficient investigation, goal achievement
