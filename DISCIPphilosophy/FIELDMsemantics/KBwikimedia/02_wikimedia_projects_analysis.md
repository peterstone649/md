# Wikimedia Projects Analysis

## Overview

This document provides a comprehensive analysis of how Wikimedia projects implement and utilize semantic technologies, examining their role as semantic portals and their impact on knowledge organization and sharing.

## Project Ecosystem

### **Core Projects**

#### **Wikipedia**
- **Role:** Free encyclopedia with semantic structure
- **Semantic Features:** Categories, infoboxes, templates, internal linking
- **Impact:** Primary semantic knowledge repository
- **Integration:** Connected to Wikidata for structured data

#### **Wikidata**
- **Role:** Central structured data repository
- **Semantic Features:** RDF export, SPARQL endpoint, linked data
- **Impact:** Semantic backbone for Wikimedia ecosystem
- **Integration:** Powers Wikipedia infoboxes and lists

#### **Wikimedia Commons**
- **Role:** Media repository with semantic metadata
- **Semantic Features:** Structured metadata, categories, licensing information
- **Impact:** Semantic organization of multimedia content
- **Integration:** Connected to Wikipedia and Wikidata

#### **Wiktionary**
- **Role:** Multilingual dictionary with semantic relationships
- **Semantic Features:** Word definitions, etymologies, translations
- **Impact:** Semantic analysis of language and meaning
- **Integration:** Linked to Wikipedia and Wikidata

### **Specialized Projects**

#### **Wikisource**
- **Role:** Digital library of source texts
- **Semantic Features:** Structured bibliographic data, author information
- **Impact:** Semantic preservation of historical documents
- **Integration:** Connected to Wikipedia biographies and historical articles

#### **Wikibooks**
- **Role:** Collection of educational textbooks
- **Semantic Features:** Structured educational content, learning objectives
- **Impact:** Semantic organization of educational materials
- **Integration:** Linked to Wikipedia for reference and expansion

#### **Wikiversity**
- **Role:** Learning materials and activities
- **Semantic Features:** Course structures, learning outcomes, assessments
- **Impact:** Semantic enhancement of educational processes
- **Integration:** Connected to Wikibooks and Wikipedia

#### **Wikiquote**
- **Role:** Collection of quotations
- **Semantic Features:** Attribution, context, source information
- **Impact:** Semantic analysis of cultural and historical quotes
- **Integration:** Linked to Wikipedia biographies and articles

## Semantic Technologies Implementation

### **Data Models**

#### **Wikidata Data Model**
- **Items:** Unique identifiers for entities (Q-numbers)
- **Properties:** Attributes and relationships (P-numbers)
- **Statements:** Claims with values, qualifiers, and references
- **Ranks:** Statement quality assessment (preferred, normal, deprecated)

#### **Wikipedia Semantic Structure**
- **Categories:** Hierarchical classification system
- **Templates:** Reusable semantic patterns
- **Infoboxes:** Structured data presentation
- **Internal Links:** Semantic relationship mapping

#### **Commons Metadata Model**
- **File Descriptions:** Structured file information
- **Categories:** Media classification and organization
- **Licensing:** Rights and usage semantics
- **Authorship:** Creator and contributor information
